Last updated:

This Privacy Policy explains how Akthomee ("I", "me", "my") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit akthomee.com.ng (the "Site"). I am committed to protecting your privacy and complying with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 and other applicable laws.

1. Information I Collect

  • Contact Form: name, email address, and the message you submit — used solely to respond to your enquiry.
  • Theme Preference: stored locally in your browser (localStorage). It never leaves your device.
  • Analytics: third-party services (such as Google Analytics) may collect anonymised usage data — pages visited, time on site, browser type.
  • Embedded Content: third-party services (such as Google Maps) may collect data about your interaction with their embedded content.

2. How I Use Your Information

  • To respond to messages sent via the contact form.
  • To understand how the Site is used so I can improve it.
  • To display location information via embedded maps.

I will not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. Cookies and Tracking

Third-party services may use cookies to track usage patterns. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some features. For details of Google's practices, see Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Sharing

Your information is shared only with trusted service providers strictly to operate the Site (contact form processing, analytics, maps), or where required by Nigerian law.

5. Data Retention

Contact form submissions are retained only as long as necessary to address your enquiry. Analytics data follows the retention policy of the provider.

6. Your Rights

Under the NDPA 2023 you have the right to access, correct, or delete any personal data I hold about you, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To exercise these rights, email hello@akthomee.com.ng.

7. Security

I take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hello@akthomee.com.ng.
